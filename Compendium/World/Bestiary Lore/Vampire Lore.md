# Vampire Lore




### Folklore Knowledge

- Vampires are Undead
-  To destroy a vampire, place a stake in it's heart, sever it's head and anoint it with _holy water_ while the stake is in place. Otherwise you must keep a vampire in sunlight until it's destroyed.
- Vampires injuries rapidly heal.
- Vampires are harmed by silver.
- A vampire can't voluntarily come within 10 feet of brandished garlic or a brandished religious symbol of a deity with a holy sanctification option. Within 10 feet weak willed vampires must flee.
- Vampires don't cast shadows or show a reflection in mirrors.
- Vampires don't usually eat or drink regular food.
- Vampires hiding their nature seem calm, persuasive, and tireless. 
- **Compulsions:**
	- A typical vampire can't voluntarily cross running water.
	- They can't enter a private dwelling unless invited in by someone with the authority to do so.
	- Vampires are fascinated with counting small scattered items like seeds or coins. 
- Very powerful vampires have more:
	- Resist physical damage except from magical silver.
	- Can turn to mist
	- Change Shape
	- Control Wild Animals

>[!info] Undead
> Once living, these creatures were infused after death with void energy and soul-corrupting unholy magic. Undead creatures are damaged by vitality energy and are healed by void energy, and don’t benefit from healing vitality effects.

>[!info] Unholy
> Tied to powerful magical forces of cruelty and sin. Creatures with this trait are often have weakness to Holy.

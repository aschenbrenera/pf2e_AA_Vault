# Chilling Darkness [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Spell 3)*

 [Attack](Attack.md "Combat Trait") [Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait") [Cold](Rules/Traits/Effect/Cold.md "Effect Trait") [Darkness](Rules/Traits/Effect/Darkness.md "Effect Trait") [Unholy](Unholy.md "Effect Trait")
**Description:** You shoot an utterly cold ray of darkness tinged with unholy energy.

**Traditions:** divine  
**Lesson:** [lesson of shadow](https://2e.aonprd.com/Lessons.aspx?ID=22)

**Range:** 120 feet
**Targets:** 1 creature
**Defense:** AC
**Activity:** Two Actions ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- Make a ranged spell attack.
	- The ray deals 5d6 cold damage. If the target has the Holy trait, you deal an extra 5d6 spirit damage.  
	  > [!success-degree]
	  > - **Critical Success** The target takes double damage.  
	  > - **Success** The target takes full damage. 
- If the ray passes through an area of magical light or targets a creature affected by magical light, _chilling darkness_ attempts to [[Counteracting|Counteract]] the light. If you need to determine whether the ray passes through an area of light, draw a line between yourself and the spell's target.

> [!info] Heighten
>- **Heightened (+1):** The cold damage increases by 2d6, and the spirit damage against holy creatures increases by 2d6.

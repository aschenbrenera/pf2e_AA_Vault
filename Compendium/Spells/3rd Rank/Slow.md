# Slow [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Spell 1)*

[Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait") 
**Description:** You dilate the flow of time around the target, slowing its actions.

**Traditions:** arcane, occult, primal 
**Bloodline*:** demonic

**Range:** 30 feet
**Targets:** 1 creature
**Defense:** Fortitude
**Duration:** Varies
**Activity:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

  > [!success-degree]
  > - **Critical Success**: The target is unaffected.
  > - **Success**: The target is [[Slowed]] 1 for 1 round.
  > - **Failure:** The target is Slowed 1 for 1 minute.
  > - **Critical Failure**: The target is slowed 2 for 1 minute.
  
> [!info] Heighten
>- **Heightened (6th):** You can target up to 10 creatures.

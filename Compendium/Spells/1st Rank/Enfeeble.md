# Enfeeble [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Spell 1)*

[Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait")
**Description:** You sap the target's strength.

**Traditions:** arcane, divine, occult 
**Catalysts:** [Vulture's Wing](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=1796)
**Patron Theme:** the resentment

**Range:** 30 feet
**Targets:** 1 creature
**Duration:** Varies
**Defense:** Fortitude
**Activity:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- Target makes a Fortitude save.
  > [!success-degree]
  > - **Critical Success**: The target is unaffected.
  > - **Success**: The target is [[Enfeebled]] 1 until the start of your next turn.
  > - **Failure:** The target is Enfeebled 2 for 1 minute.
  > - **Critical Failure**: The target is Enfeebled 3 for 1 minute.

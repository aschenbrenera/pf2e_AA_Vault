# Command [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Spell 1)*

[Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait") [Mental](Mental.md "Effect Trait") [Auditory](Rules/Traits/Effect/Auditory.md "Effect Trait") [Linguistic](Linguistic.md "Spell Trait")
**Description:** You shout a command that's hard to ignore.

**Traditions:** arcane, divine, occult  
**Patron Themes** [faith's flamekeeper](https://2e.aonprd.com/Patrons.aspx?ID=12), [the unseen broker](https://2e.aonprd.com/Patrons.aspx?ID=26), [the unseen broker](https://2e.aonprd.com/Patrons.aspx?ID=29) 

**Range:** 30 feet
**Targets:** 1 creature
**Defense:** Will
**Duration:** Until the end of the target's next turn
**Activity:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- You can command the target to approach you, run away (as if it had the [[Fleeing]] condition), release what it's holding, drop [[Prone]], or stand in place. It can't [[Delay]] or take any reactions until it has obeyed your command. The effects depend on the target's Will save.
  > [!success-degree]
  > **Success** The creature is unaffected.
  > **Failure** The creature must spend the first action on its next turn to obey your command.
  > **Critical Failure** The target must use all its actions on its next turn to obey your command.

> [!info] Heighten
>- **Heightened (5th):** You can target up to 10 creatures.

## Gameplay Interaction

- Follows the rules for the [[Auditory]] trait.
	- An action with the auditory trait can be successfully performed only if the creature using the action can speak or otherwise produce the required sounds. A spell or effect with the auditory trait has its effect only if the target can hear it.
-  Follows the rules for the [[Linguistic]] trait.
	- A linguistic effect that targets a creature works only if the target understands the language you are using.

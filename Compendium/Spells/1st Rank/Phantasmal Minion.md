# Phantasmal Minion  [](#Actions "Three-Action")  &emsp;*(Spell 1)*

[Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait") [Summon](Summon.md "Effect Trait") 
**Description:** You summon a Phantasmal Minion

**Traditions:** arcane, occult 

**Range:** 60 feet
**Duration:** Sustained
**Activity:** Three Actions ⬥⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- You summon a [[Compendium/Bestiary/Phantasmal Minion|Phantasmal Minion]].
	- The minion is roughly the shape of a humanoid.
	- You can choose to have it be invisible or have an ephemeral appearance, but it's obviously a magical effect, not a real creature.

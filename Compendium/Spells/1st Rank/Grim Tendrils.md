# Grim Tendrils [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Spell 1)*

[Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait") [Void](Void.md "Effect Trait") 
**Description:** Tendrils of darkness curl out from your fingertips and race through the air.

**Traditions:** arcane, occult 
**Catalysts:** [Void Salts](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2783)
**Mystery:** bones

**Area:** 30-foot line
**Defense:** Fortitude
**Activity:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- You deal 2d4 void damage and 1 [[Persistent Damage|Persistent]] [[Bleed Damage]] to living creatures in the line. Each living creature in the line must attempt a Fortitude save.
  > [!success-degree]
  > - **Critical Success**: The creature is unaffected.
  > - **Success**: The creature takes half the void damage and no persistent bleed damage.
  > - **Failure:** The creature takes full damage.
  > - **Critical Failure**: The creature takes double void damage and double persistent bleed damage.
  
  > [!info] Heighten
>- **Heightened (+1):** The void damage increases by 2d4, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1.

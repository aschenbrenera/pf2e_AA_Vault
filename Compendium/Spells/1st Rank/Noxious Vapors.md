# Noxious Vapors [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Spell 1)*

[Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait") [Poison](Poison.md "Effect Trait") 
**Description:** You emit a cloud of toxic smoke that temporarily obscures you from sight.

**Traditions:** arcane, primal   
**Mystery:** blight

**Area:** 10-foot emanation
**Defense:** [[Basic Saving Throw|Basic]] Fortitude
**Duration:** 1 round
**Activity:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- Each creature except you in the area when you Cast the Spell takes 1d6 poison damage wit a [[Basic Saving Throw|Basic]] Fortitude save).
	- A creature that critically fails the saving throw also becomes [[Sickened]] 1. 
- All creatures in the area become [[Concealed]], and all creatures outside the smoke become concealed to creatures within it.
- This smoke can be dispersed by a strong wind.

> [!info] Heighten
>- **Heightened (+1):** The damage increases by 1d6.

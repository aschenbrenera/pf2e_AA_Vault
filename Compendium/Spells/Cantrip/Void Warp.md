# Void Warp [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Cantrip 1)*

[Cantrip](Cantrip.md "General Trait") [Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait")  [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait") [Void](Void.md "Effect Trait")
**Description:** You call upon the Void to harm life force.

**Traditions:** arcane, divine, occult
**Bloodline:** undead
**Mysteries:** bones

**Range:** 30 feet
**Targets:** 1 living creature
**Defense:** [[Basic Saving Throw|Basic]] Fortitude
**Activity:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- The target takes 2d4 void damage with a [[Basic Saving Throw|Basic]] Fortitude save. On a critical failure, the target is also [[Enfeebled]] 1 until the start of your next turn.

> [!info] Heighten
>- **Heightened (+1):** The damage increases by 1d4.

## Gameplay Interaction

- Cantrip spells are automatically heightened to half your level rounded up.

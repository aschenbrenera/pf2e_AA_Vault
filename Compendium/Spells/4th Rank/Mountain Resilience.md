# Mountain Resilience [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Spell 4)*

 [Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait") [Earth](Earth.md "Effect Trait") 
**Description:** Targets skin hardens like the stone of a mountain face.

**Traditions:** arcane, primal   
**Catalysts:** [Dragon Scute](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=4015)

**Range:** touch
**Targets:** 1 creature
**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Cast:** Two Actions ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- The target's skin hardens. It gains resistance 5 to physical damage, except [[Adamantine]].
- Each time the target is hit by a bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing attack, _mountain resilience's_ duration decreases by 1 minute.

> [!info] Heighten
>- **Heightened (6th):** The resistance increases to 10.
>- **Heightened (8th):** The resistance increases to 15.
>- **Heightened (10th):** The resistance increases to 20.

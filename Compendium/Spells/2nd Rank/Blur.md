# Blur [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Spell 2)*

[Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait") [Illusion](Illusion.md "Effect Trait") [Visual](Visual.md "Effect Trait")
**Description:** The target's form appears blurry.

**Traditions:** arcane, occult

**Range:** touch
**Targets:** 1 creature
**Duration:** 1 minute
**Activity:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- It becomes [[Concealed]].
- As the nature of this effect still leaves the target's location obvious, the target can't use this concealment to Hide or Sneak.

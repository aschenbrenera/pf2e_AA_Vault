# Stupefy [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Spell 2)*

[Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait") [Mental](Mental.md "Effect Trait") 
**Description:** You dull the target's mind.

**Traditions:** arcane, occult

**Range:** Touch
**Targets:** 1 willing creature
**Activity:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- The target makes a Will Save.
  > [!success-degree]
  > **Critical Success:** The target is unaffected.
  > **Success:** The target is [[Stupefied]] until the start of your next turn.
  > **Failure:** The target is stupefied 2 for 1 minute.
  > **Critical Failure:** The target is stupefied 3 for 1 minute.

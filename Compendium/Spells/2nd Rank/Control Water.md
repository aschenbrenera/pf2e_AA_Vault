# Control Water [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Spell 2)*

[Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait") [Water](Water.md "Effect Trait") 
**Description:** Impose your will upon the water

**Traditions:** arcane, primal

**Range:** 500 feet
**Area:** 50 feet long by 50 feet wide
**Duration:** sustained up to 1 minute
**Defense:** Fortitude
**Duration:** 1 hour
**Cast:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- You can raise or lower the level of water in the chosen area by 10 feet.
- Creatures that have the water trait and that are in the area when you Cast the Spell must attempt a Fortitude save, with the effects of the [[Slow]] spell.
  > [!success-degree]
  > - **Critical Success**: The target is unaffected.
  > - **Success**: The target is Slowed 1 for 1 round.
  > - **Failure:** The target is Slowed 1 for 1 minute.
  > - **Critical Failure**: The target is slowed 2 for 1 minute.

# Mist [](#Actions "Three-Action") &emsp;*(Spell 2)*

[Concentrate](Concentrate.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait") [Water](Water.md "Effect Trait")
**Description:** You call forth a cloud of mist.

**Traditions:** arcane, primal 
**Catalysts:** [Gravemist Taper](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2025), [Irritating Seedpod](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=3735)
**Mystery:** [ash](https://2e.aonprd.com/Mysteries.aspx?ID=20)

**Range:** 120 feet
**Area:** 20-foot burst
**Duration:** 1 minutes
**Activity:** Three Action ⬥⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- All creatures within the mist become [[Concealed]], and all creatures outside the mist become concealed to creatures within it.
- You can Dismiss the cloud.

# Otherworldly Magic  &emsp;*(Feat 1)*

[Elf](Rules/Traits/Ancestry/Elf.md "Ancestry & Heritage Trait")
**Description:** Your elven magic manifests as a simple arcane spell, even if you aren't formally trained in magic. 
**Prerequisite:** Elf

## Mechanical Rules

- Choose one cantrip from the Arcane spell list.
	- You can cast this cantrip as an arcane innate spell at will.
		- A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.

# Titan Wrestler &emsp;*(Feat 1)*

[General](General.md "Feat Trait") [Skill](Skill.md "Feat Trait") 
**Description:** Perform strength-based attacks against larger creatures.

**Prerequisite:** Trained in [[Athletics]]

## Mechanical Rules

- You can attempt to [[Disarm]], [[Grapple]], [[Reposition]], [[Rules/Actions & Activities/Encounter/Attack/Shove]], or [[Rules/Actions & Activities/Encounter/Attack/Trip]] creatures up to two sizes larger than you, or up to three sizes larger than you if you’re legendary in Athletics.

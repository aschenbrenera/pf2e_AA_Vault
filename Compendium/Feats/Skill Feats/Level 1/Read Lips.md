# Read Lips &emsp;*(Feat 1)*

[General](General.md "Feat Trait") [Skill](Skill.md "Feat Trait") 

**Prerequisites:** Trained in [[Society]]

## Mechanical Rules

- You can read lips of others nearby who you can clearly see.
	- The language read must be one that you know.
	- When you’re at your leisure, you can do this automatically. In encounter mode or when attempting a more difficult feat of lipreading, you’re [[Fascinated]] and [[Off-Guard]] during each round in which you focus on lip movements, and you must succeed at a Society check (DC determined by the GM) to successfully read someone’s lips.

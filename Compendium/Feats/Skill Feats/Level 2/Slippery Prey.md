# Slippery Prey  &emsp;*(Feat 2)*

[General](General.md "Feat Trait") [Skill](Skill.md "Feat Trait")
**Description:** You're able to escape bonds more easily than others.

**Prerequisites:** Trained in [[Acrobatics]] or [[Athletics]]

## Mechanical Rules

-  When you attempt to [[Escape]] using Acrobatics or Athletics, you reduce the multiple attack penalty for repeated attempts to –4 and –8. 
	- The penalty becomes –3 and –6 if you're a master in the appropriate skill. If you're legendary in the skill, you don't take penalties for multiple Escape attempts in the same turn.
- Regardless of your training, your attempts to Escape still have the `Attack` trait and incur a multiple attack penalty with other actions.

# Intimidating Prowess &emsp;*(Feat 2)*

[General](General.md "Feat Trait") [Skill](Skill.md "Feat Trait")
**Description:** 

**Prerequisites:** Expert in [[Intimidation]]; Strength +3

## Mechanical Rules

- In situations where you can physically menace the target when you [[Coerce]] or [[Demoralize]], you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your Intimidation check and you ignore the penalty for not sharing a language.
- If your Strength modifier is +5 or higher and you are a master in Intimidation, this bonus increases to +2.

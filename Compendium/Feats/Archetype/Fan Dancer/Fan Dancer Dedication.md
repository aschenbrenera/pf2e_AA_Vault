# Fan Dancer Dedication &emsp;*(Feat 2)*

[Uncommon](Uncommon.md "Uncommon Rarity Trait") [Archetype](Archetype.md "Feat Trait") [Dedication](Dedication.md "Feat Trait") [Fan Dancer](Fan%20Dancer.md "Class Trait")

**Prerequisites:** Trained in [[Performance]]

**Description:** You sweep across the battlefield, manifesting both the gentle spring breeze and the crisp autumn gusts.


## Mechanical Rules

- You become an expert in Performance. 
	- At 7th level you become a master in Performance.
	- At 15th level, you become legendary in Performance. 
- Whenever you [[Feint]] while holding a fan, you can give your Feint the `Air` trait.
	- If you do, you can Stride 10 feet before or after as part of the same action.

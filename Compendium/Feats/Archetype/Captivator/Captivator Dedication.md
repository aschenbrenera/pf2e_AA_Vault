# Captivator Dedication &emsp;*(Feat 2)*

[Archetype](Archetype.md "Feat Trait") [Dedication](Dedication.md "Feat Trait") [Captivator](Captivator.md "Class Trait")

**Prerequisites:** Charisma +2; trained in [[Deception]] or [[Diplomacy]].

**Description:** Your ability to captivate others borders on the preternatural. You’re seldom at a loss in social situations, and you can create minor charms or illusions.


## Mechanical Rules

- Choose Deception or Diplomacy. You become trained in that skill or become an expert if you were already trained.
- Choose two cantrips from the [[Occult]] list. Each cantrip must have the `Illusion` trait, `Mental` trait, or both. You can cast these spells as [[Innate Spell|Innate]] occult spells.

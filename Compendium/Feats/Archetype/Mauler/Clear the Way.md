# Clear the Way [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Feat 6)*

[Archetype](Archetype.md "Feat Trait") [Mauler](Mauler.md "Class Trait")
**Description:** You put your body behind your massive weapon and swing, shoving enemies to clear a wide path.

**Prerequisites:** [[Mauler Dedication]]
**Requirements:** You’re wielding a melee weapon in two hands.
**Activity:** Two Actions ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- You attempt to [[Rules/Actions & Activities/Encounter/Attack/Shove|Shove]] up to five creatures adjacent to you, rolling a separate Athletics check for each target and ignoring the requirement that you have a hand free. 
	- Each attack counts toward your Multiple Attack Penalty, but don’t increase your penalty until you have made all your attacks.
- Then [[Stride]] up to half your Speed.
	- This movement doesn’t trigger reactions from any of the creatures you successfully Shoved.

# Basic Scroll Cache  &emsp;*(Feat 6)*

[Archetype](Archetype.md "Feat Trait") [Scroll Trickster](Scroll%20Trickster.md "Class Trait")
**Description:** You have a vast and overflowing collection of scroll scraps, riddled with errors and misspellings and leaking energy like a sieve. With enough care, you can coax these scroll scraps into functioning—briefly. 

**Prerequisites:** [[Scroll Trickster Dedication]]

## Mechanical Rules

- Each day during your daily preparations, you can create a single temporary [[Magic Scroll]] containing a 1st-rank spell. 
	- The spell must be a common spell, a spell to which you have access, or a spell you are able to cast, and you must be trained in the skill corresponding to at least one of the spell's traditions. 
	- This scroll is an unstable, temporary item and loses its magic the next time you make your daily preparations if you haven't already used it.
	- This temporary scroll can't be used to Learn the Spell.  
	- At 8th level, add a second temporary scroll containing a 2nd-rank spell.
- You can use [[Learn Spell]] to add spells to those you can use with this feat, even if you aren't a spellcaster.

# Hunter's Aim [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Feat 2)*

[Ranger](Ranger.md "Class Trait") [Concentrate](Concentrate.md "General Trait")
**Description:** When you focus on aiming, your attack becomes particularly accurate. 

**Requirements:** You are wielding a ranged weapon with reload 0.
**Activity:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- Make a ranged weapon Strike against your hunted prey.
	- On this Strike, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll and ignore your prey’s concealed condition and any lesser cover.

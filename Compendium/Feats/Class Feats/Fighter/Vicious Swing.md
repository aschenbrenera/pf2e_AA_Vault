# Vicious Swing [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Feat 1)*

[Fighter](Fighter.md "Class Trait") [Flourish](Flourish.md "Combat Trait")
**Description:** You unleash a particularly powerful attack that clobbers your foe but leaves you a bit unsteady.

**Activity:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- Make a melee Strike.
	- This counts as two attacks when calculating your multiple attack penalty.
	- If this Strike hits, you deal an extra die of weapon damage.
		- If you’re at least 10th level, increase this to two extra dice, and if you’re at least 18th level, increase it to three extra dice.

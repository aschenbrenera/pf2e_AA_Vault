# Parting Shot [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Feat 4)*

[Fighter](Fighter.md "Class Trait")
**Description:** You jump back and make a quick shot that catches your opponent by surprise.

**Requirements:** You are wielding a loaded ranged weapon or a ranged weapon with reload 0.
**Activity:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- You Step and then make a ranged Strike with the required weapon. Your target is [[Off-Guard]] against the attack.
- This feat can be used for one or more archetypes in addition to the listed classes. When selected this way, the feat does not have its class traits.

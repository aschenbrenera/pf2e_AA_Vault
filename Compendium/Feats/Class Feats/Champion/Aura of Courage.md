# Aura of Courage &emsp;*(Feat 4)*

[Champion](Champion.md "Class Trait")
**Description:** You stand strong in the face of danger and inspire your allies to do the same. 

**Prerequisites:** [[Champion's Aura]], Holy

## Mechanical Rules

- Whenever you become [[Frightened]], reduce the condition value by 1 (to a minimum of 0).
- At the end of your turn, each ally in your champion's aura reduces its frightened value by 1.

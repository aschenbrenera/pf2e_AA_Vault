# Mug [](#Actions "Two-Action") &emsp;*(Feat 4)*

[Rogue](Rules/Traits/Class/Rogue.md "Class Trait")
**Description:** You make a quick attack and rob your foe in the process. 

**Activity:** Two Action ⬥⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- Make a melee Strike against an adjacent enemy.
	- If you hit and deal sneak attack damage, you can also attempt to [[Steal]] from the target, even if the target is in combat.

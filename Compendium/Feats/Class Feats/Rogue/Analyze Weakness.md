# Analyze Weakness [](#Actions "Single Action") &emsp;*(Feat 6)*

[Rogue](Rogue.md "Class Trait")
**Description:** Your knowledge of a creature’s physiology helps you attack with pinpoint accuracy. You carefully study a creature that you’ve identified to scope out particularly weak points in its positioning or physical form.

**Prerequisites:** [[Sneak Attack]] 2d6
**Requirements:** You have identified a creature with Recall Knowledge.
**Activity:** Single Action ⬥

## Mechanical Rules

- Your choose a creature that you've identified with Recall Knowledge.
- The next time you deal sneak attack damage to the chosen creature with a Strike before the end of your turn, add an additional 2d6 precision damage. 
	- At 11th level, the additional damage becomes 3d6, and at 17th level it becomes 4d6.

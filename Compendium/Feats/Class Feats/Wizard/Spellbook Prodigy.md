# Spellbook Prodigy &emsp;*(Feat 1)*

[Wizard](Wizard.md "Class Trait")
**Description:** You are particularly adept at learning spells to add to your spellbook. 
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Arcana]]

## Mechanical Rules

- You gain the [[Magical Shorthand]] skill feat without needing to meet its prerequisites. 
- Further, when you roll a critical failure on your check to [[Learn Spell]], you get a failure instead.

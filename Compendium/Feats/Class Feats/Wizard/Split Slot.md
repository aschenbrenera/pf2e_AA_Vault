# Split Slot &emsp;*(Feat 6)*

[Wizard](Wizard.md "Class Trait")
**Description:** You can prepare two spells in one slot, giving you the freedom to choose the spell when you cast it. 

## Mechanical Rules

- When you prepare your spells for the day, you can choose one spell slot at least 1 rank lower than the highest-rank spell you can cast and prepare two spells in that slot. 
- When you Cast a Spell from that slot, choose which spell to cast.
	- Once you’ve chosen, the unused spell dissipates as though you hadn’t prepared it at all. The unused spell isn’t available for use with Drain Bonded Item.

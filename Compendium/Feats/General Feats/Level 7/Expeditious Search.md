# Expeditious Search &emsp;*(Feat 7)*

[General](General.md "Feat Trait") 
**Description:** You have a system that lets you search at great speed, finding details and secrets twice as quickly as others can.

**Prerequisite:** Master in [[Perception]].

## Mechanical Rules

- You take half as long to [[Search]] a given area, doubling how much grand you can cover (up to half your Speed). This grants the following benefits:
	- If your standard search speed exceeds 300 feet per minute (**Speed 30**), you can't ensure you check everything before you walk into it; ([[Detecting Hazards and Traps]]).
	- If your standard search speed exceeds 600 feet per minute (**Speed 60**), you can't search thoroughly enough to guarantee you checked *everything*.
- If you have the [[Thorough Search]] feat, it grants the following benefits as well:
	- If your thorough search speed exceeds 150 feet per minute (**Speed 15**), you can't ensure you check everything before you walk into it; (Detecting Hazards and Traps).
	- If your thorough search speed exceeds 300 feet per minute (**Speed 30**), you can't search thoroughly enough to guarantee you checked *everything*.


> [!tip] Tabletop Interpretation
> This feat if for very niche scenarios. Most games hand wave search time, and it only really helps mounted or very fast characters with a base speed of 40 or higher, racing a clock to find something hidden.

# Trackless Journey

[Ranger](Ranger.md "Class Trait")
**Description:** When you move through natural terrains, you are hard to track. 

## Mechanical Rules

- You always gain the benefits of the [[Cover Tracks]] action in natural terrains, without moving at half your Speed.

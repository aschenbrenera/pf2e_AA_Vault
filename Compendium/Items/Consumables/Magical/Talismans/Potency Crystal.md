# Potency Crystal &emsp;*(Item 1+)*

[Consumable](Consumable.md "Item Trait") [Magical](Magical.md "Item Trait") [Talisman](Talisman.md "Item Trait")

**Bulk:** —
**Usage:** Affixed to a weapon
**Activate:**  [](#Actions "Single Action") (concentrate); **Trigger:** You make an attack with the affixed weapon, but you haven't rolled yet.

**Description:** This fluorite crystal glows with a strange phosphorescence. 


| **Level** | **Item** | **Price** | Effect                     |
| --------- | -------- | --------- | -------------------------- |
| 1         | Potency Crystal | 4 gp      | The weapon becomes a +1 striking weapon on the Strike and until the end of this turn, gaining a +1 item bonus to the attack roll and increasing the damage to two weapon damage dice. |
| 9         |  Potency Crystal (Greater) | 150 gp    | The weapon becomes a +2 greater striking weapon on the Strike and until the end of this turn, gaining a +2 item bonus to the attack roll and increasing the damage to three weapon damage dice. |
| 15        |  Potency Crystal (Major)    | 1,300 gp    | The weapon becomes a +3 major striking weapon on the Strike and until the end of this turn, gaining a +3 item bonus to the attack roll and increasing the damage to four weapon damage dice. |

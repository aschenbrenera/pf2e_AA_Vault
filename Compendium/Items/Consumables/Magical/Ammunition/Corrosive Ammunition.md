# Corrosive Ammunition &emsp;*(Item 3)*

[Consumable](Consumable.md "Item Trait") [Magical](Magical.md "Item Trait") [Acid](Acid.md "Effect Trait") 

**Price:** 70 gp
**Bulk:** —
**Ammunition:** Any
**Activate:** [](#Actions "Single Action") (manipulate)

This peculiar ammunition is coated in yellow dust that leaves a stain on anything it touches.

## Mechanical Rules

- When activated _corrosive ammunition_ hits a target, it dissolves across the target's armor. The armor takes 1d8 [[Persistent Damage|Persistent]] acid damage that ignores the armor's Hardness.
- If the target isn't wearing armor, it takes the acid damage instead. This damage occurs at the end of the target's turns.  
- The creature can end this effect by spending an Interact action to wipe off the corrosive dust. Otherwise, the effect ends once the armor becomes Broken.

## Gameplay Notes

- The damage from  _corrosive ammunition_ is a secondary effect and not part of the strike. The damage is not modified by critical success.

# Ghost Touch Rune

 [Magical](Magical.md "Item Trait")

**Bulk:** —
**Usage:** etched onto a weapon

A weapon etched with this rune can harm creatures without physical form.


| **Level** | **Item**           | **Price** | Effect      |
| --------- | --------           | --------- | ----------  |
| 4         | Ghost Touch        | 75 gp    | A _ghost touch_ weapon is particularly effective against [[Incorporeal]] creatures, which almost always have a specific vulnerability to ghost touch weapons. Incorporeal creatures can touch, hold, and wield ghost touch weapons (unlike most physical objects).  |

# Fearsome Rune

 [Magical](Magical.md "Item Trait") [Fire](Rules/Traits/Effect/Fire.md "Effect Trait") 

**Bulk:** —
**Usage:** etched onto a weapon

A weapon with this rune is empowered by flickering flame.


| **Level** | **Item**           | **Price** | Effect      |
| --------- | --------           | --------- | ----------  |
| 8         | Flaming            | 500 gp    | The weapon deals an additional 1d6 fire damage on a successful Strike, plus 1d10 Persistent fire damage on a critical hit.<br>**Craft Requirements** You're an expert in Crafting.  |
| 15        | Flaming  (Greater) | 6,500 gp  | The weapon deals an additional 1d6 fire damage on a successful Strike, plus 2d10 Persistent fire damage on a critical hit. Fire damage dealt by this weapon (including the persistent fire damage) ignores the target's fire resistance.<br>**Craft Requirements** You're an expert in Crafting. |

  ## Gameplay Interaction

- You can upgrade a rune already etched on a weapon using the normal process for [upgrading items and runes](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=3157).

# Mage's Hat

[Invested](Invested.md "Item Trait") [Arcane](Magical.md "Effect Trait")
**Description:** This hat comes in many forms, such as a colorful turban or a pointy hat with a brim, and is adorned with symbols or runes. 

**Bulk:** —
**Usage:** worn headwear

| **Level** | **Item**               | **Price** | **Effect** |
| --------- | ----------------------      | --------- | ----------------- |
| 3         | Mage's Hat  | 50 gp | It grants you a +1 item bonus to Arcana checks and allows you to cast the [[Prestidigitation]] cantrip as an arcane innate cantrip.|
| 9         | Mage's Hat (Greater)| 650 gp | This larger, fancier hat grants a +2 bonus and can be activated. Each greater mage's hat has a specific 4th-rank summon spell from the arcane list woven into its fabric, typically [_summon animal_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=316) or [_summon elemental_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=320). If you prepare arcane spells, you can change the spell to a different 4th-rank arcane summon spell you know when you invest it. **Activate—Hat Spell** Cast a Spell; **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You doff the hat, causing magical energy to pour from it. You cast the spell stored in the hat.

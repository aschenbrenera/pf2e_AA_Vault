# Weapon Tricks

[Rogue](Rules/Traits/Class/Rogue.md "Class Trait")
**Description:** As someone who takes advantage of openings, you are careful not to leave such gaps yourself.

## Mechanical Rules

- You gain expert proficiency in simple weapons, martial weapons, and unarmed attacks. 
- When you critically succeed at an attack roll against an [[Off-Guard]] creature while using an [[Agile]] or [[Finesse]] weapon or unarmed attack, you apply the Critical Specialization for that weapon or unarmed attack.

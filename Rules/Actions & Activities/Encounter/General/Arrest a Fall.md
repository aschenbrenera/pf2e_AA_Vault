# Arrest a Fall (basic) [](#Actions "Reaction")

**Description:** You press yourself against a wall or duck behind an obstacle to take better advantage of cover. 

**Trigger:** You fall.
**Requirements:** You have a [[Fly Speed]].
**Activity:** Reaction ⤾

## Mechanical Rules

- You attempt your choice of an [[Acrobatics]] check or Reflex save to slow your [[Falling|Fall]]. The DC is typically 15, but it might be higher due to air turbulence or other circumstances.
> [!success-degree] 
>- **Success:** You take no damage from the fall.

# Construct

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar
**Description:** Your familiar is a construct.
**Requirements:** Your familiar must have the [[Tough]] ability to select this.

## Mechanical Rules

- Your familiar has the [[Construct]] trait instead of the `Animal` trait.
	- The familiar is immune to:
		- `Death` effects
		- `Disease`
		- `Doomed`
		- `Drained`
		- `Fatigued`
		- `Healing`
		- `Nonlethal` attacks
		- `Paralyzed`
		- `Poison`
		- `Sickened`
		- `Spirit`
		- `Unconscious`
		- `Vitality`
		- `Void`

# Elemental

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar
**Description:** Your familiar is a construct.
**Requirements:** Your familiar must have the [[Resistance Familiar Ability]] to select this.

## Mechanical Rules

- Your familiar has the [[Elemental]] trait instead of the `Animal` trait.
	- Choose `Air`, `Earth`, `Fire`, `Metal`, `Water` or `Wood`. Your familiar gains that trait. 
	- The familiar is immune to:
		- `Bleed`
		- `Paralyzed`
		- `Poison`
		- `Sleep`
		- the element matching its (chosen) trait.

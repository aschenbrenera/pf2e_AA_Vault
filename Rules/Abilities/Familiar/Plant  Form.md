# Plant Form

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar
**Requirements:** You must have a familiar with the [[Plant]] trait to select this ability.

## Mechanical Rules

- Your plant familiar can change shape as a single action, transforming into a Tiny plant of a type roughly similar to the familiar's nature. 
- This otherwise uses the effects of [[One with Plants]].

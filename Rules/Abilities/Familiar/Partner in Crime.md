# Partner in Crime

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar
**Description:** Your familiar is your criminal associate.

## Mechanical Rules

- Despite being a minion, your familiar gains 1 reaction at the start of its turns, which it can use only to Aid you on a Deception or Thievery skill check (it still has to prepare to help you as normal for the Aid reaction).
- It automatically succeeds at its check to Aid you with those skills or automatically critically succeeds if you're a master of the skill in question.

# Accompanist

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar
**Description:** Your familiar helps you perform. Your familiar accompanies you with chirps, claps, or its own miniature instrument. 

## Mechanical Rules

- Whenever you attempt a [[Performance]] check, if your familiar is nearby and can act, it grants you a +1 circumstance bonus, or +2 if you're a master in Performance.

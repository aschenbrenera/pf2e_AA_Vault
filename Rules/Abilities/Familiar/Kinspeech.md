# Kinspeech

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar
**Requirements:**
- Your familiar must be an `Animal`.
- Your familiar must have the [[Speech]] familiar ability to select this.
- You must be at least 6th level.

## Mechanical Rules

- Your familiar can understand and speak with animals of the same species.

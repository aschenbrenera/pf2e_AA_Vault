# Spellcasting

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar
**Requirements:** You must be able to cast 6th-rank spells using spell slots to select this.

## Mechanical Rules

- Choose a spell in your repertoire or that you prepared today at least 5 ranks lower than your highest-rank spell slot.
	- Your familiar can Cast that Spell once per day using your magical tradition, spell attack modifier, and spell DC.
	- If the spell has a drawback that affects the caster, both you and your familiar are affected.

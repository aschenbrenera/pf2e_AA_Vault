# Resistance

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar

## Mechanical Rules

- Choose two of the following: `Acid`, `Cold`, `Electricity`, `Fire`, `Poison`, or `Sonic`. 
	- Your familiar gains [[Resistance]] equal to half your level (minimum resistance 1) against the chosen damage types.

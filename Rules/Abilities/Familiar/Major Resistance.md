# Major Resistance

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar
**Requirements:** 
- Your familiar must have the [[Resistance Familiar Ability]] to select this.
- You must be at least 8th level.

## Mechanical Rules

- Your familiar increases the resistance it gains from its Resistance Familiar Ability to a value equal to your level.

# Damage Avoidance

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar

## Mechanical Rules

-  Choose one type of save (when your familiar takes this). Your familiar takes no damage when it rolls a success on that type of save; this doesn't prevent effects other than damage.

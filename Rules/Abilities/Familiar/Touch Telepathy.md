# Touch Telepathy

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar

## Mechanical Rules

- Your familiar can telepathically communicate with you via touch.
- If it also has the [[Speech]] ability, it can telepathically communicate via touch with any creature if they share a language.

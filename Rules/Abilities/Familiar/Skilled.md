# Skilled

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar

## Mechanical Rules

- Choose a skill other than Acrobatics or Stealth. 
	- Your familiar's modifier for that skill is equal to your level plus your spellcasting attribute modifier, rather than just your level. 
- You can select this ability repeatedly, choosing a different skill each time.

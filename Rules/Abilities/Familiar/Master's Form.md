# Master's Form

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar
**Requirements:** Your familiar must have the [[Manual Dexterity]] and [[Speech]] familiar abilities to select this.

## Mechanical Rules

- Your familiar can change shape as a single action, transforming into a `Humanoid` of your ancestry, but with the same age, gender, and build of its true familiar form.
	- It always maintains a clearly unnatural remnant of its nature, such as a cat's eyes or a serpent's tongue. 
	- This form is always the same each time it uses this ability. 
	- It only appears humanoid and gains no new capabilities and doesn't change statistics in any way.
	- This otherwise uses the effects of [[Humanoid Form]], except the change is purely cosmetic. 
	    - The familiar gains the [[Humanoid]] trait  while in this form, as well as trait related to your ancestry (such as `Goblin` or `Human`).

> [!tip] Tabletop Interpretation
> The wording of Master's Form creates an ambiguity concerning the familiar's apparent size and which mechanical effects of _Humanoid Form_ carry over.
> 
> **RAW — Original size:** The familiar retains its original size. The familiar does not necessarily gain the +4 status bonus to Deception from the _Humanoid Form_ spell.
> 
> **Alternative interpretation — Master's apparent size:** The familiar's apparent size matches the master's size, but the size change is purely cosmetic and provides none of the mechanical benefits associated with changing Size. The familiar may gain the +4 status bonus to Deception from the _Humanoid Form_ spell.

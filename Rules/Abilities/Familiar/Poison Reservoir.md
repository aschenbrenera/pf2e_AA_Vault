# Poison Reservoir

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar
**Requirements:** You must have a [[Homunculus]] familiar to select this ability.

## Mechanical Rules

- Your familiar has a reservoir for poison, allowing it to apply an injury poison to an adjacent ally’s exposed weapon with a single Interact action. 
	- You must supply the poison and instill it into this reservoir using two consecutive Interact actions.

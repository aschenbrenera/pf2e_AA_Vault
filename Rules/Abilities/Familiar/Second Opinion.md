# Second Opinion

**Type:** [[Pet, Familiar, and Master Abilities|Familiar Ability]] - Familiar
**Description:** Your familiar is your academic confidant.
**Requirements:** Your familiar must have the [[Skilled]] familiar ability to select this.

## Mechanical Rules

- Despite being a minion, your familiar gains 1 reaction at the start of its turns, which it can use only to Aid you on a Recall Knowledge skill check for a skill in which it has the skilled familiar ability (it still has to prepare to help you as normal for the Aid reaction). 
- It automatically succeeds at its check to Aid you with those skills or automatically critically succeeds if you’re a master of the skill in question.

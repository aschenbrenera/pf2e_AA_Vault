# Spontaneous Spell

Spontaneous spells provide you with more freedom in your spellcasting, but you have fewer spells in your spell repertoire, as determined by your character level and class.

You choose which spell from your spell repertoire that you’re using a spell slot for at the moment you decide to cast it. 

When you make your daily preparations, all your spell slots are refreshed, but you don’t get to change the spells in your repertoire.

## Mechanical Rules

- You don't have to prepare spontaneous spells. You select a spell and spell slot at the moment you cast it.
- Spontaneous spell slots are refreshed during your [[Rest#Daily Preparations|Daily Preparations]].

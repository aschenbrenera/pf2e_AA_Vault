# Consumable

Consumable items include alchemical items and magical consumables such as scrolls and talismans.

## Mechanical Rules

- An item with this trait can be used only once.
- Unless stated otherwise, it’s destroyed after activation. 
- A character can Craft consumable items in batches of four.

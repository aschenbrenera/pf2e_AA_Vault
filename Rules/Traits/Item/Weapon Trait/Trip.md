# Trip

## Mechanical Rules

- You can use this weapon to [[Rules/Actions & Activities/Encounter/Attack/Trip|Trip]] with the Athletics skill even if you don't have a free hand.
	- This uses the weapon's reach (if different from your own).
	- This adds the weapon's item bonus to attack rolls as an item bonus to the Athletics check.
	- If you critically fail a check to Trip using the weapon, you can drop the weapon to take the effects of a failure instead of a critical failure.

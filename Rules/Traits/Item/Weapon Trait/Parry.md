# Parry

This weapon can be used defensively to block attacks.

## Mechanical Rules

- While wielding this weapon, if your proficiency with it is trained or better, you can take the [[Rules/Actions & Activities/Encounter/Defend/Parry|Parry]] action.
	- Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.

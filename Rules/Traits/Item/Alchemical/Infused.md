# Infused

An [[Alchemical]] item with the infused trait has a limited time before it becomes inert. 

## Mechanical Rules

- Any non-permanent effects from your infused alchemical items, with the exception of afflictions such as slow-acting poisons, end when you make your daily preparations again.

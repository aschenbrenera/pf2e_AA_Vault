# Talisman

A talisman is a small [[Magical]] object [[Affix a Talisman|Affixed]] to armor, a shield, or a weapon (called the affixed item). 

## Mechanical Rules

- You must be wielding or wearing an item to activate a talisman attached to it.
- Once activated, a talisman burns out permanently.

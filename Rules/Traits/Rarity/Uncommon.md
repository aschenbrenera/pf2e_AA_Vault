# Uncommon

Something of uncommon rarity requires special training or comes from a particular culture or part of the world. Some character choices give access to uncommon options, and the GM can choose to allow access for anyone. Less is known about uncommon creatures than common creatures.

## Mechanical Rules

- The DC of Recall Knowledge checks related to Uncommon creature is increased by 2.
- Uncommon creatures typically can't be summoned.

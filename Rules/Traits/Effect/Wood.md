# Wood

Effects with the wood trait conjure or manipulate wood. Creatures with this trait consist primarily of wood or have a connection to magical wood.  
  
These planes consist of trees and other flora latticed in organic patterns. Wood planes are often perfectly constructed to match their desired purpose or environment but aren’t usually outright hostile to visitors.

## Mechanical Rules

 - Those that manipulate wood have no effect in an area without wood.

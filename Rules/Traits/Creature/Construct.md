# Construct

A construct is an artificial creature empowered by a force other than vitality or void. Constructs are often mindless;  Constructs are not living creatures, nor are they undead. Constructs might have Hardness based on the materials used to construct their bodies.

## Mechanical Rules

- When reduced to 0 Hit Points, a construct creature is destroyed.
- Constructs are immune to: bleed damage, disease, healing, nonlethal attacks, poison, vitality, void, and the doomed, drained, fatigued, paralyzed, sickened, and unconscious conditions.

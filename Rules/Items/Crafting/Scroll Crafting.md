# Scroll Crafting - Guide

[Downtime](Downtime.md "Action & Ability Trait") [Manipulate](Manipulate.md "General Trait")
**Description:** You can make a [[Magic Scroll]].

**Skill:** [[Crafting]] (Trained)
**Requirements:** You have the [[Magical Crafting]] skill feat.

## Mechanical Rules

### Setting Up to Craft

- To craft a magic scroll, you must also meet the following requirements:
	- The scroll item level is your level or lower. 
		- If the item is 9th level or higher, you must be a master in Crafting.
		- If the item is 16th or higher, you must be legendary in Crafting.
	- **Scroll Materials:** A scroll requires a suitable writing medium and writing implements, and at least half the scroll (or batch) price in gold. Someone participating in the crafting must cast the spell(s).
		- Narratively, the crafting cost includes magical inks, reagents, components, and other consumable materials required to bind and stabilize the spell. The higher the spell's rank, the greater the gold investment required, even though the finished scroll may appear physically similar to a lower-rank scroll.
- You can choose to create a scroll with a heightened spell. For example, it is possible to create a 4th-Rank spell of Fireball.
- Scrolls can be crafted in batches of up to four. All scrolls of one batch must contain the same spell at the same rank, and you must provide one casting for each scroll that is crafted.

### Crafting Process

- You always expend raw materials worth at least half the scroll's price when you Craft successfully.
- You attempt a Crafting check after you spend 2 days of work setting up, or 1 day if you have the [[Formulas|Formula]] for Magic Scroll. The GM determines the DC, but base DCs are listed in the Scroll Overview table. 
  > [!success-degree]
  >- **Critical Success**: Your attempt is successful. Each additional day spent Crafting reduces the materials needed to complete the item by an amount based on your level + 1 and your proficiency rank in Crafting.
  >- **Success**: Your attempt is successful. Each additional day spent Crafting reduces the materials needed to complete the item by an amount based on your level and your proficiency rank.
  >- **Failure**: You fail to complete the item. You can salvage the raw materials you supplied for their full value. If you want to try again, you must start over.
  >- **Critical Failure**: You fail to complete the item. You ruin 10% of the raw materials you supplied, but you can salvage the rest. If you want to try again, you must start over.
- If your attempt to create the item is successful, you expend the raw materials you supplied. You can pay the remaining portion of the item's Price in materials to complete the item immediately, or you can spend additional downtime days working on it.
	- For each additional day you spend, reduce the value of the materials you need to expend to complete the item. This amount is determined using the Income Earned (or 'Crafting Value' below) table, based on your proficiency rank in Crafting and using your own level instead of a task level.  
	  - After any of these downtime days, you can complete the item by spending the remaining portion of its price in materials. If the downtime days you spend are interrupted, you can return to finish the item later, continuing where you left off. 

### Example

A 6th level wizard with Expert Crafting and the Magical Crafting feat can craft up to a 3rd-rank Magic Scroll. This wizard wants to create three 3rd-rank scrolls of Floating Flame (a 2nd-rank spell being heightened). The batch of 3 scrolls would normally cost 90 gp. The wizard will have to supply half of the batch cost to begin, 45 gp.
This wizard has the formula for Magic Scroll (applies to all spell ranks), so they can make a crafting check after spending one day crafting (otherwise they would spend two days crafting before making the check).
The GM sets the DC at 20 (the base DC for 3rd-rank scrolls). The player checks the degree of success of their crafting check. If they get a success or critical success, they can choose to spend the other half of the batch cost (another 45 gp) to immediately create the scrolls, or they can use additional days to reduce the remaining cost (no further checks necessary). Assuming the wizard's check was 22 (a success), they can reduce the remaining cost of the batch by 2gp (Level 6 x Crafting Expert), for each additional day spent crafting. The Wizard spent 6 days crafting (1 initial and 5 to reduce), spending a total of 80 gp to create three 3rd-rank scrolls of Floating Flame. The same wizard could spend three days creating four 1st-rank scrolls for 6 gp with a successful check, or spend six days creating a single 3rd-rank scroll for 15 gp with a critical successful check.

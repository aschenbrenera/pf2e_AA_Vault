# Spellcasting Items

|Item type|How it works|Main advantage|Main limitation|Best use|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**[[Scroll]]**|One-time casting of a specific spell|**Cheapest and most flexible** way to carry extra spells|Consumed; spell must be on your spell list|Emergency spells, niche spells, high-rank one-offs|
|**[[Wand]]**|Specific spell, normally once/day; can overcharge|**Reliable daily extra spell**|Only one spell; normally once/day; overcharging risks destruction|A spell you expect to use almost every day|
|**Staff**|Multiple spells; spend charges to cast them|**Most spells per item** and excellent daily flexibility|Must be able to cast spells of the appropriate rank; spell must be on your spell list; requires preparation|Broad spell selection|
|**[[Spellheart]]**|Affix to armor/weapon; activation casts its specific spell|**Adds magic to equipment you're already using**|Usually much more specialized than a wand/staff|Martial characters or casters wanting an extra combat ability|

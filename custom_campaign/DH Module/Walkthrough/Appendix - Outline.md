# “Death House” – Narrative Outline

##  Overview
A gothic horror adventure set in a cursed house that lures travelers with the promise of salvation, but slowly reveals a history of cultic ritual, murder, and betrayal. This outline tracks the major plot beats of the player’s journey, with special callouts for story scenes that unveil the house’s grim history.

---

## 1. Arrival at the House

- **Plot Beat:** Party is told to investigate disappearances. They are told the disappearances are connected to a strange mist and a mysterious house.
  
- ** Story Scene – *The Gnome Traveler***
  - **What the Players Learn:** There is a house that appears in the mist that is connected to the disappearances.

---

## 2. Initial Exploration: The Illusion of Normalcy

- **Plot Beat:** The party explores elegant but eerie rooms. They begin to sense something is wrong through minor hauntings and out-of-place items.

- **🗝️ Story Scene – *The Library’s Secrets***
  - **What the Players Learn:** Hidden cult documents describe the Durst family’s involvement in a death cult.
  - **Reveals:** The house hides a legacy of ritualistic evil beneath its polished surface.

---

## 3. Climbing Toward the Past: The Attic

- **Plot Beat:** The players ascend into the attic, discovering long-abandoned rooms and tragic remnants of the Durst family.

- **🗝️ Story Scene – *Rose and Thorn’s Room***
  - **What the Players Learn:** The children’s skeletal remains are locked in their room.
  - **Reveals:** They starved to death. Their spirits may possess players until properly buried.

- **🗝️ Story Scene – *The Nursemaid’s Secret***
  - **What the Players Learn:** The ghostly nursemaid haunts the attic. Her remains are hidden.
  - **Reveals:** Elizabeth Durst had a child (Walter) for sacrificial purposes. The cult turned on her.

---

## 4. Descent Into Darkness: The Secret Stairwell

- **Plot Beat:** A hidden passage leads downward from the attic to the crypts and cult dungeon beneath the house.

---

## 5. The Cult Dungeon

- **Plot Beat:** The players explore decaying chambers, twisted halls, and sacrificial altars. Tension builds as they approach the truth.

- **🗝️ Story Scene – *Cultist Chambers and Echoes***
  - **What the Players Learn:** Rooms used for rituals and imprisoning victims give context to the cult’s practices.
  - **Reveals:** The cult demanded regular sacrifices to a dark entity and turned on the Dursts when unsatisfied.

- **🗝️ Story Scene – *The Crypts***
  - **What the Players Learn:** Empty crypts bear the names of the Dursts and their children.
  - **Reveals:** Rose, Thorn, and the nursemaid must be buried here to find peace. The house resents this kindness.

---

## 6. Final Confrontation: The Ritual Chamber

- **Plot Beat:** The players find the central ritual chamber where the cult tried to summon a dark being.

- **🗝️ Story Scene – *The Cult Demands a Sacrifice***
  - **What the Players Learn:** A spectral voice demands a living sacrifice.
  - **Reveals:** Refusing the demand triggers the final boss fight.

- **⚔️ Final Encounter – *Lorgoth the Decayer***
  - **What the Players Confront:** A corrupted mass of flesh and nature, spawned by the failed rituals.
  - **Reveals:** The true horror birthed by the cult’s hubris. A fight for survival follows.

---

## 7. Escape from the Dying House

- **Plot Beat:** The house reacts violently to the ritual’s disruption. Players must flee collapsing rooms and deadly traps.

- **🗝️ Story Scene – *The House Strikes Back***
  - **What the Players Learn:** The house becomes hostile—filling with smoke, slamming doors, and activating traps.
  - **Reveals:** The house is a living entity, punishing those who defy its will.

---

## 8. Resolution: Sunlight and Fog

- **Plot Beat:** Survivors emerge from the house back into Barovia’s misty realm.

- **🗝️ Story Scene – *The Children’s Rest (Optional)***
  - **What the Players Learn:** If Rose and Thorn’s remains were buried, their spirits are freed.
  - **Reveals:** Even in darkness, compassion matters—and may briefly push back the curse.

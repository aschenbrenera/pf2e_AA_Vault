# The Aleister Order of Occult Investigation

## GM Reference Document

**This document contains information intended only for the GM.**

---

# Threat Classification

The Order categorizes supernatural incidents by severity.

### Class I — Folklore

Unconfirmed rumors, minor hauntings, or unusual events.

### Class II — Manifestation

Confirmed supernatural activity or a dangerous entity.

### Class III — Predatory

A supernatural threat actively harming or hunting civilians.

### Class IV — Catastrophic

A threat capable of destroying settlements or causing widespread death.

### Class V — Cataclysmic

A threat that challenges the natural order itself.

---

# The Aleister Oath

Every warden swears:

> I seek truth despite fear.  
> I shield the weak.  
> I carry knowledge from darkness.


---

# Field Protocol

When arriving at a new investigation, wardens follow the Order's standard procedure:

## 1. Observe

Determine what happened.
- Collect evidence.  
- Speak with witnesses.  
- Identify inconsistencies.

## 2. Investigate

Determine the nature of the threat.
- What caused this?  
- Are there motives?
- How can it be stopped?

## 3. Protect

Prevent further harm.
- Evacuate civilians.  
- Secure dangerous objects.  
- Contain the threat.

## 4. Report

Every investigation becomes part of the Order's archives.

# The True Nature of the Order

The Aleister Order presents itself as a scholarly investigative organization dedicated to protecting civilization from supernatural threats.

That is true.

However, the Order is not as clean or heroic as its public image suggests.

The organization has existed for centuries because the supernatural world is far larger, stranger, and more dangerous than most people realize.

The Order survives because it understands one important truth:

**Some horrors cannot be destroyed. They can only be studied, contained, and watched.**

---

# The Order's Internal Philosophy

The Order is divided over one central question:

## Should supernatural threats be destroyed or understood?

Some members believe:

> "Every monster is a danger until proven otherwise."

Others believe:

> "A thing understood is a thing controlled."

This creates internal conflict between:

- Researchers who want knowledge.
- Wardens who want containment.
- Hunters who want elimination.

The PCs may encounter members from all three philosophies.

---

# The Hidden History

The Order's official records claim it was founded to protect humanity from supernatural threats.

The truth is more complicated.

The original founders were not merely hunters.

They were survivors.

The first members formed the Order after encountering an event so dangerous that entire communities were erased from history.

The founders discovered that some entities cannot be killed.

They can only be:

- Bound.
- Hidden.
- Forgotten.

The Order's archives contain many secrets that even most members are forbidden from accessing.

---

# The Black Archive

The most secret division of the Order is known internally as the Black Archive.

Its purpose:

- Study impossible phenomena.
- Store dangerous artifacts.
- Record knowledge too dangerous for common access.

The Black Archive contains:

- Cursed relics.
- Forbidden rituals.
- Names of ancient entities.
- Failed investigations.

Some members believe the Black Archive protects the world.

Others believe it is a collection of disasters waiting to happen.

---

# Why the PCs Receive These Missions

The Order is not large enough to respond personally to every incident.

Most members are researchers, archivists, or administrators.

Field teams are valuable because they combine:

- Combat ability.
- Investigation skills.
- Independence.
- Adaptability.

The PCs are trusted because they are capable of handling situations where ordinary investigators would fail.

---

# The Order's Limitations

The Order cannot simply solve every problem because:

## Resources Are Limited

Supernatural incidents are increasing faster than the Order can respond.

## Information Is Incomplete

Many threats are poorly understood.

Old records may be inaccurate.  
Witnesses may be unreliable.  
Previous agents may have disappeared.

## Some Threats Are Protected

Powerful individuals, governments, and noble houses may conceal supernatural activity.

The Order often operates without official support.

# Long-Term Campaign Mystery Ideas

The Order itself can become part of the campaign mystery.

Possible revelations:

## The Founder

The Order's founder may not have been entirely human.

## The Archives

Some case files may have been altered or erased.

## The Artifacts

The Order's collection of dangerous objects may be attracting supernatural attention.

## The Investigations

Many unrelated cases may connect to the same ancient force.

# Recommended Tone

The Aleister Order should feel like:

- A detective agency investigating impossible crimes.
- A monster hunting organization with academic roots.
- A candlelit archive full of forbidden knowledge.
- A group of people trying to protect the world while knowing too much about it.

The Order should never feel completely safe.

The PCs should believe:

"We are the people who stand between civilization and the dark."

But occasionally wonder:

"How much darkness have we brought home with us?"

## Adventure Overview

The PCs arrive around **8:00 PM** at the Valemort Estate several hours before midnight. The masquerade begins immediately. They are welcomed as honored guests and encouraged to enjoy the masquerade. Midnight is the fixed deadline.

The investigation should gradually reveal three truths:

1. **The guests cannot leave.**
2. **The hosts are vampires, and the masquerade is a harvest.**
3. **The manor's supernatural protections can be turned against its masters.**

The players should have opportunities to discover weaknesses that directly affect the final confrontation.

The adventure should not require the PCs to discover every clue. Once they understand the central threat and have enough information to act, move the adventure toward midnight.



The Valemort Masquerade is not a story about hunting vampires.
It is a story about entering a beautiful place and slowly realizing that the monsters are not hiding, they are hosting a part and the guests are the ones who are trapped.


The blood offered during the ritual accumulates within the Blood Fountain. When the ritual is completed, the fountain grants the coven a temporary supernatural boon, empowering them far beyond their normal abilities.

# Case Briefing

## Incident Report

For decades, a mysterious masquerade has appeared once every year at the abandoned Valemort Estate.

Each year:

- Wealthy citizens receive invitations.
- Guests attend the celebration.
- The guests disappear.
- No bodies are recovered.

The event has been dismissed as rumor due to a lack of evidence.

Three days ago, a survivor appeared.

The survivor:

- Claims they attended the masquerade only one week ago.
- Has not aged despite being missing for 40 years.
- Cannot remember how they escaped.
- Begs the Order to investigate before the next gathering.

---

## Primary Objectives

- Confirm supernatural activity.
- Identify the cause of the disappearances.
- Prevent further victims.

## Secondary Objectives

- Determine the identity of the event organizers.
- Recover evidence for the Order archives.
- Rescue surviving victims if possible.
- Recover any dangerous supernatural artifacts.

# Location: Valemort Estate

An ancient noble manor abandoned after the Valemort family died under mysterious circumstances.

Despite being abandoned:

- The gardens are perfectly maintained.
- Candlelight burns in every window.
- Music can be heard from inside.
- Servants prepare for guests who have not arrived.

The manor appears beautiful from a distance.

Up close, the illusion begins to fail.

---

# Act 1: The Invitation to the Dead

## Goal

Introduce the mystery and establish that something is deeply wrong.

## Runtime

30–45 minutes

---

# Arrival

The PCs arrive at Valemort Estate shortly before midnight.

A servant opens the door before they knock.

The servant says:

> "Welcome. The Master has been expecting you."

The PCs are treated as honored guests.

They are given:

- Masks
- Wine
- Ballroom invitations

They are never asked for names.

The estate already knows who they are.

---

# Initial Investigation

The players may explore before entering the ballroom.

## Discoverable Clues

### The Guests

The attendees appear wealthy and elegant.

Strange details:

- Nobody eats.
- Nobody drinks.
- Nobody leaves.
- Nobody removes their masks.

---

### The Mirrors

Mirrors throughout the estate show strange reflections:

- Empty rooms.
- Missing guests.
- Figures standing behind the PCs.

Eventually, the PCs notice:

The vampires have no reflections.

---

### The Portrait Gallery

Paintings depict the guests.

The problem:

The paintings are decades old.

Many guests are shown exactly as they appear today.

---

### The Servants

The servants:

- Never speak unless spoken to.
- Have no pulse.
- Cannot explain how long they have worked there.

---

# Social Encounters

The PCs can interact with guests.

## The Desperate Guest

A mortal noble secretly approaches the PCs.

They reveal:

- They came willingly.
- They thought they were attending a normal celebration.
- They cannot remember arriving.
- They believe the doors will not open again.

---

## The Vampire Duelist

A vampire noble challenges a PC to a "friendly" dance.

The duel is polite and elegant.

The vampire is testing:

- Combat ability.
- Self-control.
- Whether the PCs are worth turning.

---

## The Ghostly Musician

A ghostly orchestra plays throughout the evening.

A hidden investigation reveals:

The musicians died decades ago.

They are forced to perform every year.

---

# Act 2: The Truth Behind the Masquerade

## Goal

Reveal the vampire court and give players meaningful preparation before the final battle.

## Runtime

45–60 minutes

---

# The Midnight Ritual

The PCs discover the truth:

The masquerade is not a party.

It is a harvest.

Every year, the vampire court invites mortals to select one worthy individual.

That person becomes the newest member of the vampire nobility.

The remaining guests become:

- Thralls.
- Food.
- Decorations for the eternal celebration.

---

# The Vampire Court

## Lady Seraphine Valemort

The host of the masquerade.

An ancient vampire noble obsessed with elegance and immortality.

She does not see herself as evil.

She believes vampirism is an elevation beyond mortal weakness.

---

## The Court

The vampires represent different forms of corruption:

### The Duelist

Pride.

Believes strength determines worth.

### The Confessor

Control.

Uses secrets and emotional manipulation.

### The Patron

Greed.

Offers power and immortality.

---

# Investigation Objectives

Before midnight, players can discover ways to weaken the vampires.

## Objective 1: Break the Blood Fountain

The magical fountain powers the vampire court.

Effect:

- Vampires regenerate.
- The manor remains protected.

---

## Objective 2: Open the Sun Chambers

The ballroom windows are sealed by magic.

Players can:

- Disable magical locks.
- Break protective runes.
- Redirect mirrors.

---

## Objective 3: Recover the Valemort Relic

A holy artifact belonging to the original family is hidden in the manor.

It can weaken Lady Seraphine.

---

# Act 3: The Blood Waltz

## Goal

Final confrontation.

## Runtime

60–90 minutes

---

# Midnight

The clock strikes twelve.

The music stops.

The guests remove their masks.

Most of them are revealed to be:

- Dead.
- Thralls.
- Undead servants.

Lady Seraphine welcomes the PCs.

She offers them immortality.

If refused:

The Blood Waltz begins.

---

# Final Battlefield

## Location

The Grand Ballroom

---

# Battlefield Features

## Blood Fountain

The source of the vampires' power.

Effects:

- Heals vampire creatures.
- Strengthens vampire abilities.

Players can destroy it.

---

## Chandeliers

Can be dropped.

Effects:

- Damage creatures below.
- Create difficult terrain.
- Block movement.

---

## Mirrors

The mirrors contain false images.

Effects:

- Create vampire duplicates.
- Reveal hidden movement.

Destroying mirrors weakens the vampires.

---

## Magical Dance Floor

The ballroom itself is enchanted.

Effects:

- Forces movement.
- Creates difficult terrain.
- Separates characters.

---

## Sealed Windows

Initially unusable.

Players must spend actions opening them.

Once opened:

- Sunlight enters.
- Vampires lose advantages.

# Resolution

## Success

The PCs:

- Defeat the vampire court.
- Rescue surviving victims.
- Recover evidence.

The Aleister Order records the case as:

**Case File #0174 — Confirmed Vampire Court**

## Failure

If Lady Seraphine escapes, The Order learns:
The vampire court was only one branch of a much larger network.
The masquerade continues elsewhere.

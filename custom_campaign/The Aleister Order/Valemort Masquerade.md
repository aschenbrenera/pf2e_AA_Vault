# Valemort Masquerade Overview

## Adventure Title

The Valemort Masquerade

## Theme

Gothic vampire horror

The Valemort Masquerade is not a story about hunting vampires. It is a story about looking for the abducters of missing people, and ending up a party before realizing that the party is hosted by monsters. 

## Tone

- Decadent aristocratic horror
- Mystery hidden beneath beauty
- Ancient evil wearing a civilized mask
- The fear of discovering the monster was invited

## Recommended Party

4 PCs at level 10

## Enemies

1 [Vampire Count](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3225) (joins fight after servitor is dead)
1 [Vampire Servitor](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3224) (joins fight after 3 servants are dead)
8 [Vampire Servant](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3858)

## Expected Runtime

3–4 hours (Rapid Single-Session Adventure)

## Adventure Structure

- Investigation Briefing — 10 minutes
- Arrival and Initial Investigation — 30–45 minutes
- Investigation and Preparation — 30–45 minutes
- Midnight Revelation — 10–15 minutes
- Final Ballroom Confrontation — 45–60 minutes
- Resolution and Report — 10 minutes

## Location: Valemort Estate

The Valemort Estate has been officially abandoned for 50 years, following the disappearance of the Valemort family.

The estate remains remarkably well preserved despite its supposed abandonment. Its gardens are carefully maintained, its furnishings remain in excellent condition, and the manor shows signs of regular use.

During the investigation, the PCs may discover evidence of this maintenance:
- Freshly polished silver
- Carefully tended gardens
- Rooms that appear to have been recently occupied

The estate contains extensive underground chambers that are not visible from the main manor. These chambers serve as the coven's private quarters and storage.

## The Adventure from the Monster's Perspective

For the last 40 years, a moroi vampire coven has secretly occupied the abandoned Valemort Estate. Every four years, the coven hosts the Valemort Masquerade: an elaborate celebration and a ritual honoring Zura, the vampire goddess of blood and cannibalism.

The coven carefully selects mortals from Lepidstadt and the surrounding region and sends them invitations. The Vampire Servitor is responsible for delivering the invitations and selecting the coven's guests. Approximately 15 guests are invited to every ball. The ritual requires 4 victims to empower the coven. The 'stronger' the blood of the sacrifices the stronger the blessing. This leads the coven to pick as wealthy or as powerful sacrifices as they can manage without drawing too much attention. Another two guests are personally claimed by the Vampire Count and his servitor, who feed upon them. The remaining guests are lightly drained and returned home that same night, their memories altered with the 'Rewrite Memory' spell. 

The masquerade provides the coven with a banquet of blood and it fulfills the ritual required to invoke Zura's blessing. Zura's blessing grants the vampires additional strength and powers. Notably powerful vampires, such as the Vampire Count temporarily gain the ability to cast Rewrite Memory as an Innate Spell on anyone whose blood it's just tasted. This is how the coven wipes the memories of the victims it releases.

The coven's secrecy has endured because the masquerade occurs only once every four years, the victims willingly attend, and those who do return don't remember it. The disappearances are widely regarded as unrelated incidents or local folklore.

### The Blood Rite

At midnight, four selected guests are taken to a ritual basin called 'The Blood Pool' and positioned around it. Four blood channels connect the sacrifices to the pool. Once the rite begins, the victims are bled over six rounds. At the end of the sixth round, the accumulated blood invokes Zura and grants the coven its blessing.

The Wardens can learn during the investigation that the rite depends upon all four sacrifices remaining in position and a small unholy relic affixed to the basin. During the final encounter, the rite should function as a six-round objective rather than a background event. The Wardens can interrupt it by rescuing a sacrifice, removing the relic, or otherwise interfering with the Blood Pool. The vampires and their servants attempt to prevent this while the rite progresses.

### A survivor and the hook

Eight years ago, the Vampire Servitor took one of the masquerade guests as a personal pet rather than killing them. The servitor repeatedly altered the victim's memories, eventually leaving them unable to retain memories formed after their original attendance at the masquerade. During preparations for this year's masquerade, the victim escaped the estate carrying one of the invitations intended for one of this year's guests.

The survivor believes they attended the masquerade only recently and cannot meaningfully explain the intervening eight years. Their condition and the genuine invitation provide the Order with its first reliable evidence connecting the disappearances to the Valemort Estate.

The Order assigns the Wardens to investigate the survivor and use the recovered invitation to attend the masquerade. The vampires do not know that the invitation is being used by Wardens until the investigation is underway.

# Case File #0174: Lepidstadt Disappearances - OPEN

**Provisional Threat Class:** Class III — Predatory  
**Incident Type:** Suspected Abduction Activity  
**Location:** The region around Lepidstadt

### Report

For 40 years we've received reports from the region surrounding Lepidstadt that some people go missing every four years. Each time it happens, it seems about half a dozen people go missing all around the same time.

28 years ago we sent a team to investigate, but were only able to substantiate that people had gone missing over the years. That year 7 more people went missing, all within the same day or two. The team investigated for 2 weeks without uncovering any additional information.

12 years ago, after many more disappearances, we received a communication from a prominent merchant in the area who claimed to have received an invitation that day for a masquerade ball the following night, but he was to afraid too attend. We sent a second team to investigate, but the merchant had died shortly after sending the communication. The invitation was never found. The team confirmed 6 people in the area went missing the week of the merchant's death.

### New Evidence

Three days ago, a human survivor appeared in Lepidstadt. Records confirm that he disappeared 8 years ago, but the survivor claims to have received an invitation to the masquerade "last night". He seems to have no memory of the last 8 years.
The survivor was found carrying an invitation. The invitation bears the following inscription. 
> The Valemort Estate _requests the pleasure of your company_ tomorrow evening at the Valemort Masquerade.
> An evening of music, revelry, and indulgence beneath the light of the full moon. The celebration will culminate at midnight with the final dance.
> Masks required. 
> Please exercise the utmost discretion, as this is a private affair. 

Researchers have confirmed that the disappearances correlate with the first night of a full moon. ("The next occurrence is tonight." - Written in the margin)

### Mission Objectives

**Primary:**
- Determine the nature of the phenomenon.
- Prevent further disappearances.

**Secondary:**
- Recover any cursed or otherwise dangerous objects.

**Optional:**
- Locate and rescue any surviving victims.

# Case Agent Briefing

## Agent Elias Voss

Elias Voss is an experienced Aleister field agent who now serves primarily as a regional case handler. Voss is calm, professional, and direct. 

## Meeting Location

The PCs meet Voss at the Aleister Order's small field office in Lepidstadt. The office is a modest rented townhouse used by the Order for regional investigations.

Voss provides any necessary clarification regarding the Case File and answers the PCs' questions before the investigation begins.

## Information Not Included in the Case File

Voss can answer the following questions.

### Why now?

The survivor's appearance. For the first time, the Order has both a confirmed connection to the phenomenon and a genuine invitation to the upcoming Masquerade.

Voss believes this may be the Order's best opportunity to investigate the actual event rather than the abandoned estate.

### Who is the survivor?

The survivor's identity has been confirmed. He was a banker who disappeared eight years ago. He has no memory before 8 years ago. He seems to lose any new memories each time he awakes. On the last day of his memory from 8 years ago, he awoke to find an invitation slipped beneath the door. He says the invitation found on him seems different than the one he remembers, but can't say why.

The Order believes the survivor's memories have been altered or damaged by supernatural means.

## Mission

Voss's instructions are straightforward:
> Attend the Masquerade. Investigate the disappearances. 
> If there is a malevolent force behind the disappearances that you can neutralize, proceed to do so. Otherwise, collect information for the case file and safely return to report back. 

## Transition to Valemort Estate

Voss arranges a carriage to the estate. The journey takes approximately 3 hours and does not need to be played out.

The roads become narrower and less traveled. Eventually, the carriage enters old woodland. The driver remains silent and does not acknowledge questions. After several hours, the carriage stops before an iron gate. Beyond it stands the Valemort Estate. Every window glows with candlelight. Music drifts from somewhere beyond the gates. Servants wait at the entrance. A servant approaches, bows, and says:
> "Welcome. You are the last to arrive."

# Act 1: The Masquerade

**Goal:** Establish the mystery and make the PCs realize they are trapped.

The PCs arrive around **8:00 PM** at the Valemort Estate several hours before midnight. The masquerade begins immediately. Each PC who isn't wearing one receives a mask and is treated as an honored guest. Nobody asks their names. Four of the vampires are dressed as  servants. The remainder of the vampires are dressed as guests. The PCs find that aside from themselves, the ball has less than 20 masked attendees. A somewhat small intimate affair in a large dining hall cleared out for the party. 

## Act 1 Clues

- A guest who tries to leave discovers that the doors will not open.
- Certain guests don't seem to eat or drink.
- The mirrors show inconsistent reflections.

Possible discoveries:

- Figures appear where nobody is standing.
- Rooms appear empty when they are occupied.
- Some guests have no reflection.
- The PCs may occasionally see something standing behind them.

### The Portrait Gallery

The portraits depict members of the Valemort household and previous guests.

Several paintings are decades old, yet depict people who appear unchanged.

### The Servants

The servants are unnaturally quiet and never seem to rest.

They have no pulse.

They cannot remember when they began serving the Valemorts.

## Social Encounters

These encounters are optional and should provide clues rather than become mandatory scenes.

### The Desperate Guest

A mortal guest secretly approaches the PCs.

They reveal:
- They came willingly.
- They cannot remember arriving.
- The doors will not open.
- They believe something terrible will happen at midnight.

### The Vampire Duelist

A vampire noble invites a PC to dance.

The encounter is polite, elegant, and unsettling. The vampire is testing the PCs' abilities and temperament.

The vampire may offer cryptic warnings rather than immediately attacking.

### The Ghostly Orchestra

The musicians have performed at the masquerade for decades.

Investigation reveals that they died long ago and are bound to the estate.

# Act 2: The Harvest

**Goal:** Reveal the truth and give the PCs time to prepare.

The PCs should eventually discover that the masquerade is a supernatural trap.

The guests are not attending a party.

**They are being harvested.**

At midnight, the vampire court selects one or more guests for transformation. Others become food, thralls, or permanent residents of the estate.

The PCs are unusual guests because the vampires do not yet know whether they are potential victims, rivals, or something more interesting.

## Lady Seraphine Valemort

Lady Seraphine is the mistress of the estate and leader of the vampire court.

She is ancient, elegant, intelligent, and completely convinced that vampirism is an elevation beyond mortal weakness.

She does not consider herself a monster.

She considers herself a host.

Seraphine should remain polite until the PCs openly threaten the court.

At some point before midnight, she offers the PCs immortality.

> "Why cling to a life that ends?"

## The Court

Use only as many court members as the adventure needs.

### The Duelist

A proud vampire who believes strength determines worth.

### The Confessor

A manipulative vampire who collects secrets and uses them to control others.

These vampires can serve as social encounters, combatants, or both.

# Investigation Discoveries

The investigation should lead toward three useful discoveries.

The PCs do not need to find all three.

## The Blood Fountain

A supernatural fountain beneath the manor is fed by the blood of previous victims.

It sustains the vampire court and protects the estate.

**If destroyed:** vampires lose a major source of healing/power and the estate's supernatural protections weaken.

## The Sun Chambers

The ballroom's windows are sealed by magical wards.

The PCs can discover how to disable the wards, allowing sunlight into the ballroom.

**If opened:** portions of the ballroom become dangerous or inaccessible to the vampires once sunlight enters.

## The Valemort Relic

A sacred relic belonging to the original Valemort family is hidden within the manor.

The relic is harmful to Seraphine and can weaken her supernatural defenses.

The relic's exact nature can be determined during play.

# The Midnight Revelation

At midnight, the clock strikes twelve.

The music stops.

The guests remove their masks.

Many are revealed to be:

- Vampires
- Thralls
- Undead servants
- Long-dead guests preserved by the manor

The illusion of the masquerade ends.

Lady Seraphine addresses the PCs directly and formally welcomes them to the "true" celebration.

She offers them immortality one final time.

If they refuse, the Blood Waltz begins.

# Act 3: The Blood Waltz

**Goal:** Defeat the vampire court and break the Masquerade.

The final battle takes place in the Grand Ballroom.

The ballroom should feel like the culmination of everything the PCs discovered.

## Battlefield Features

### Blood Fountain

Provides power to the vampire court.

If the PCs discovered and disabled it, the vampires lose its benefits.

### Chandeliers

Can be dropped to damage creatures, create difficult terrain, or divide the battlefield.

### Mirrors

Create false images and obscure the vampires' movements.

Destroying or manipulating them can weaken the court.

### Magical Dance Floor

The floor is enchanted and subtly forces movement according to the rhythm of the music.

Use this primarily to reposition or separate characters rather than constantly impose complicated effects.

### Sunlight

If the PCs opened the Sun Chambers, sunlight enters the ballroom.

Create areas where the vampires cannot safely remain.

# Final Encounter

## Lady Seraphine Valemort

**Primary Boss**

Role:

- Controller
- Manipulator
- Final villain

Seraphine should use the ballroom and its supernatural features rather than simply trading attacks with the PCs.

## Vampire Lieutenant

**Elite Threat**

Use either the Duelist or Confessor as Seraphine's primary lieutenant.

The other may flee, surrender, or become a secondary complication depending on pacing.

## Vampire Spawn

**Supporting Enemies**

Use sparingly.

Their purpose is to occupy the PCs and reinforce the sense that the entire court has turned against them.

## Charmed Guests

Some surviving guests remain under the court's control.

The PCs may attempt to protect or free them during the battle.

Do not make saving them mandatory for victory.

# Pacing

The adventure should naturally accelerate toward midnight.

If the players are moving quickly, allow them to discover several clues and proceed directly to the finale.

If the players are taking too long, have the clock advance or have Seraphine initiate the midnight ceremony.

Do not require every investigation scene to be completed.

The essential progression is:

**The guests are trapped → The hosts are vampires → The masquerade is a harvest → The PCs discover a weakness → Midnight → Final confrontation.**

# Resolution

## Success

The PCs defeat or drive off the vampire court and prevent the night's harvest.

Possible accomplishments:

- Surviving guests are rescued.
- The Valemort Estate is secured.
- The Blood Fountain is destroyed.
- The Valemort Relic is recovered.
- Previous investigators' records are recovered.

The Order updates the case:

**Case File #0174 — Confirmed Vampire Court**

## Partial Success

The PCs prevent the immediate massacre but fail to destroy the court.

Seraphine or another vampire escapes.

The Order considers the case unresolved.

This can establish a recurring antagonist.

## Failure

The vampire court survives and the masquerade continues.

If Seraphine escapes with knowledge of the PCs, the Order may later discover that the Valemort Masquerade was only one part of a larger network of supernatural courts.

This should remain a future plot hook rather than an immediate revelation.

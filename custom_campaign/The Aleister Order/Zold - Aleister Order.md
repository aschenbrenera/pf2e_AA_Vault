# The Aleister Order of Occult Investigation

 "We stare into the darkness and never blink."

## Who We Are

The Aleister Order is an organization dedicated to investigating supernatural threats, unexplained events, and dangerous occult phenomena.

For a century, the Order has operated in quiet obscurity, protecting ordinary people from dangers they could neither confront or understand.

Where others dismiss rumors and superstition, the Aleister Order investigates.

## Our Purpose

The Aleister Order exists to:

- Investigate supernatural anomalies before they become disasters.
- Identify and contain dangerous entities.
- Protect communities from occult threats.
- Recover and secure cursed or dangerous objects.
- Record knowledge about supernatural phenomena for future generations.

The Order's duty is to determine when a strange occurrence becomes a threat.

# Wardens

Wardens are members of the Order's field division.

Wardens are investigators, explorers, and monster hunters trained to enter dangerous situations and uncover the truth.

A deployment of wardens typically come in threes:

###  Lanterns

Specialists in magic and knowledge.

###  Seekers

Specialists in uncovering secrets with guile, infiltration, and charm.

### Shields

Specialists in combat.

## Responsibilities

All warden's responsibilities include:

### Investigation

Gather evidence.  
Interview witnesses.  
Study locations.  
Determine the nature of the threat.

### Containment

Prevent supernatural threats from spreading.  
Protect civilians.  
Secure dangerous objects or locations.

### Resolution

When a threat cannot be contained, wardens have the authority to eliminate it.
Create a report for the order.

# What the Order Provides

As an Aleister warden, you have access to:

## Case Files

Records of previous investigations, known creatures, and historical incidents.

## Training and Expertise

The Order provides instruction in:

- Monster identification
- Occult investigation
- Magical phenomena
- Survival techniques
- Threat assessment

## Resources

Wardens may receive:

- Financial compensation
- Equipment support
- Official credentials
- Assistance from other Order members

## Authority

An Aleister badge grants recognition as an official investigator of supernatural events.

Many communities distrust outsiders, but most know that when the Order arrives, something unusual has occurred.

# The Order's Reputation

Among ordinary people, the Aleister Order is known as:

- Investigators of strange events
- Protectors against hidden dangers
- Experts in matters beyond common understanding

Some people view the Order with gratitude. Others fear them.

# The Order and the Supernatural

The Order does not assume that every supernatural being is an enemy.

Some are dangerous.

Some are hostile.

Some are simply misunderstood.

Some may be reasoned with.

Others cannot be reasoned with at all.

Your responsibility is to determine which is which.

Do not mistake ignorance for wisdom.

Do not mistake curiosity for caution.

And never assume that because something is ancient, magical, or powerful, it is therefore wise.

---

# The Order's Reputation

The Aleister Order is not equally known everywhere.

Some communities welcome Wardens as protectors.

Others are suspicious of outsiders investigating their affairs.

Some consider the Order little more than eccentric scholars.

Others believe that if the Aleister Order has arrived, something terrible must be happening.

Do not expect a particular reaction.

Earn trust where you can.

---

# What We Expect From You

You are not treasure hunters.

You are not mercenaries.

You are not executioners.

You are investigators of the impossible.

You are protectors against the unknown.

You are custodians of knowledge that may one day save a life.

There will be times when you must fight.

There will be times when you must run.

There will be times when you must make decisions without knowing whether they are correct.

The Order does not expect perfection.

It expects judgment.

# Your First Case

When you receive a Case File, read it carefully.

Speak with the supervising agent.

Ask questions.

Determine what resources you require.

Then go.

Investigate the circumstances described in the file.

Find out what is actually happening.

Protect those who need protection.

Resolve the threat.

Return with your report.

And remember:

> 
> **Go find it.**

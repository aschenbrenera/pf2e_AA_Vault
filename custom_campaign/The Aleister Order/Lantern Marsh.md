The Lantern Marsh is not a story about killing ghosts.

It is a story about discovering that the monsters haunting the marsh were once people who were afraid of being forgotten.


# Case File #0181: The Lantern Marsh

### Aleister Order Classification

**Threat Classification:** Class II — Manifestation  

### Incident:

**The Lantern Road Disappearances**

### Classification:

Unconfirmed supernatural abduction activity

### Location:

The Blackwater Marsh and surrounding trade routes

### Report:

For generations, travelers crossing the old Lantern Road have disappeared without explanation. Witnesses report strange lights drifting above the marsh after sunset, often followed by voices calling travelers by name.

Those who follow the lights vanish into the fog and are never seen again.

Previous investigations classified the disappearances as accidents caused by dangerous terrain and poor visibility.

A recent Aleister field agent investigation has provided new evidence of supernatural involvement.

The investigator returned three days after entering the marsh despite having been missing for nearly two weeks. They were found covered in unfamiliar symbols and unable to explain where they had been.

Before their death, they delivered a final statement:

> "The village beneath the water is still waiting for its guests."

### Mission Objectives

**Primary:**

- Determine the source of the ghostly lanterns.
- Identify the entity responsible for the disappearances.
- Prevent further loss of life along the Lantern Road.

**Secondary:**

- Recover the missing investigator's research.
- Locate previous victims.
- Determine the origin of the submerged settlement.
- Recover any cursed or supernatural artifacts.

**Optional:**

- Free trapped spirits within the marsh.
- Restore the lost shrine beneath the water.

# Case Briefing

## Incident Report

For generations, travelers along the old Lantern Road have disappeared while traveling through the Blackwater Marsh.

Local reports describe:

- Strange lights appearing above the water.
- Voices calling travelers by name.
- People walking willingly into the fog.
- Travelers disappearing without leaving tracks behind.

The Aleister Order previously classified the incidents as isolated disappearances caused by dangerous terrain.

A recent investigation has changed that assessment.

An Aleister field agent entered the marsh to investigate the phenomenon.

They returned three days later.

They were soaking wet, covered in strange symbols, and unable to explain where they had been.

Before collapsing, they delivered one warning:

> "The village beneath the water is still waiting for its guests."

The investigator died shortly afterward.

## Primary Objectives

- Determine the source of the Lantern Marsh phenomenon.
- Identify the supernatural entity responsible.

- Locate the missing travelers.
- Recover the previous investigator's research.
- Determine the origin of the ghostly lanterns.
- Recover any cursed or supernatural artifacts.

# Location: Blackwater Marsh

An ancient wetland surrounding the remains of a forgotten settlement.

The marsh was once a major trade route.

Now it is avoided by travelers.

---

## The Marsh Environment

The Blackwater Marsh contains:

- Thick fog that obscures vision.
- Half-submerged ruins.
- Twisted trees growing from flooded streets.
- Old stone paths disappearing beneath the water.
- Strange lanterns drifting across the surface.

The deeper the PCs travel, the less the marsh resembles a natural place.

---

# Act 1: The Lantern Road

## Goal

Introduce the mystery and establish that the marsh is actively drawing people toward it.

## Runtime

30–45 minutes

---

# Arrival

The PCs arrive at a settlement near the edge of the marsh.

The villagers are fearful but reluctant to discuss the disappearances.

They know:

- The lanterns appear after sunset.
- Those who follow them rarely return.
- The voices in the fog sometimes sound like loved ones.

---

# Initial Investigation

## The Missing Travelers

The PCs investigate recent disappearances.

Evidence reveals:

- Travelers did not flee.
- Campsites were left intact.
- Personal belongings remain behind.
- Footprints lead into the marsh and stop at the water.

---

## The Missing Investigator's Camp

The PCs discover the abandoned camp of the previous Aleister agent.

Recovered notes reveal:

- References to a submerged settlement.
- Drawings of ancient symbols.
- Reports of hearing voices beneath the water.

The final entry reads:

> "The dead are not hunting us. They are inviting us."

---

## The Ghost Lanterns

The PCs encounter the mysterious lights for the first time.

The lanterns:

- Move deliberately.
- Avoid approaching too closely.
- Appear to guide travelers.
- Respond to spoken names.

The players must decide whether to follow them.

---

# Act 2: The Drowned Village

## Goal

Reveal the tragedy behind the haunting and discover what created the Lantern Marsh.

## Runtime

45–60 minutes

---

# The Submerged Settlement

Following the lanterns leads the PCs to the remains of an entire village beneath the marsh.

The ruins include:

- Homes preserved beneath the water.
- A flooded marketplace.
- A collapsed chapel.
- A submerged shrine.

The village appears frozen at the moment of its destruction.

---

# The Drowned

The PCs encounter the undead residents of the lost village.

These creatures are not mindless.

They:

- Speak of their homes.
- Ask the PCs to stay.
- Believe the village is still alive.
- Do not understand why outsiders fear them.

---

# Exploration Locations

## The Sunken Chapel

The PCs discover:

- Records of the village's history.
- The original pact that protected the settlement.
- Evidence of the disaster that destroyed it.

---

## The Broken Shrine

The PCs learn:

The village once made a bargain with an ancient marsh spirit.

The agreement provided:

- Protection from storms.
- Safe passage through the wetlands.
- Prosperity for generations.

The price was eventually demanded.

The villagers attempted to break the pact.

The marsh claimed them all.

---

# The Lantern Widow

The PCs discover the identity of the spirit maintaining the haunting.

The Lantern Widow was once the village guardian.

In life:

- She guided travelers safely through the marsh.
- She maintained the shrine.
- She protected the village.

In death:

- She believes the village has been abandoned.
- She calls travelers home.
- She refuses to allow anyone else to be forgotten.

---

# Investigation Objectives

Before confronting the Lantern Widow, the PCs can discover ways to weaken the curse.

---

## Objective 1: Recover the Village Bell

The chapel bell still carries the village's original blessing.

It can weaken the spirits.

---

## Objective 2: Restore the Shrine

Repairing the shrine allows the trapped souls to find peace.

---

## Objective 3: Learn the Truth of the Pact

Understanding what happened gives the PCs leverage over the Lantern Widow.

They may be able to resolve the conflict without destroying her.

---

# Act 3: The Drowned Shrine

## Goal

Confront the source of the haunting and decide the fate of the forgotten village.

## Runtime

75–90 minutes

---

# The Final Confrontation

The PCs descend into the submerged shrine beneath the marsh.

The Lantern Widow awaits.

She does not immediately attack.

She asks:

> "Will you leave us forgotten again?"

If the PCs cannot convince her that the village will be remembered, combat begins.

---

# Final Battlefield

## Location

The drowned shrine beneath Blackwater Marsh.

The battlefield is partially submerged and constantly changing.

---

# Battlefield Features

## Rising Water

Throughout combat:

- Water levels increase.
- Movement routes change.
- Lower areas become dangerous.
- Characters must reposition.

---

## Ghost Lanterns

Floating lights move throughout the battlefield.

Effects:

- Create illusions.
- Reveal hidden paths.
- Teleport spirits.
- Manipulate visibility.

Players can extinguish or redirect them.

---

## The Broken Bell Tower

A damaged bell hangs above the shrine.

Players can:

- Ring the bell to weaken undead creatures.
- Cause the structure to collapse.
- Use it as a battlefield hazard.

---

## Drowned Memories

The shrine forces visitors to experience fragments of the past.

Characters may witness:

- The village before the disaster.
- The failed attempt to break the pact.
- The Lantern Widow's final moments.

These memories reveal the tragedy behind the haunting.

---

# Enemy Encounter

## The Lantern Widow

### Role

Primary supernatural threat.

### Combat Style

- Battlefield control.
- Fear and emotional manipulation.
- Water-based attacks.
- Summoning drowned spirits.

The Lantern Widow is not cruel.

She is a protector who no longer understands that her protection has become a curse.

---

## Supporting Enemies

### Drowned Guardians

Former villagers who defend the shrine.

Role:

- Protect the Lantern Widow.
- Control movement.
- Create environmental pressure.

---

## Optional Threat

### The Marsh Itself

The environment becomes hostile.

Possible effects:

- Grasping hands from the water.
- False voices calling characters away.
- Sudden flooding.
- Illusory paths.

---

# Resolution

## Success

The PCs:

- Free the trapped spirits.
- End the disappearances.

The Clockmaker's House is not a story about defeating a mad inventor.
It is a story about a man who loved his family so much that he refused to accept death.

# Case File #0192: The Clockmaker's House

### Aleister Order Classification

**Threat Classification:** Class II — Manifestation  

### Incident:

**The Voss Clockworks Incident**

### Classification:

Unlicensed magical experimentation / artificial life manifestation

### Location:

The abandoned Voss Estate and underground clockwork workshop

### Report:

Six months ago, renowned artificer and clockmaker Alistair Voss withdrew from public life following the death of his family.

Shortly afterward, unusual mechanical creations began appearing throughout the surrounding region.

Reported incidents include:

- Clockwork animals replacing ordinary wildlife.
- Mechanical servants appearing in abandoned homes.
- Household objects repairing themselves overnight.
- Machines repeating the names of deceased citizens.
- Strange mechanical activity originating from the sealed Voss workshop.

Initial investigations classified these events as unauthorized magical craftsmanship.

New evidence suggests the creations are not simple machines.

Several constructs demonstrate:

- Personal memories.
- Emotional responses.
- Knowledge of individuals who are deceased.

The Order believes Voss may have discovered a method of preserving consciousness through artificial vessels.

His workshop remains sealed.

No one who has entered in the last three months has returned.

### Mission Objectives

**Primary:**

- Investigate the Voss workshop.
- Determine the nature and threat level of the clockwork creations.
- Contain or neutralize any dangerous artificial lifeforms.

**Secondary:**

- Recover Voss's research notes.
- Determine the source of the transferred memories.
- Secure any dangerous magical technology.
- Assess whether the creations possess true consciousness.

**Optional:**

- Rescue surviving creations.
- Determine whether Alistair Voss can be safely apprehended.
- Recover evidence regarding forbidden resurrection research.

# Case Briefing

## Incident Report

Master Alistair Voss was once considered one of the greatest clockmakers and artificers in the region.

Six months ago, following the death of his family, Voss withdrew from public life and sealed his workshop.

Recently, unusual mechanical creations have begun appearing throughout the city.

Reports include:

- Clockwork animals replacing real animals.
- Mechanical servants appearing in abandoned homes.
- Household objects repairing themselves overnight.
- Machines repeating the names of deceased citizens.
- Strange sounds coming from the sealed Voss workshop.

The Order initially classified these events as unauthorized magical experimentation.

New evidence suggests the creations are not simply machines.

They possess memories.

---

# Location: Voss Clockworks

A massive mechanical workshop hidden beneath the abandoned Voss estate.

Once a place of invention and craftsmanship, it has become a maze of:

- Moving machinery.
- Strange experiments.
- Forgotten memories.
- Unfinished creations.

The workshop continues operating despite Voss no longer receiving visitors.

---

# Act 1: The Silent Workshop

## Goal

Introduce the mystery and reveal that the machines are not behaving normally.

## Runtime

30–45 minutes

---

# Arrival

The PCs arrive at the Voss estate.

The building appears abandoned.

However:

- The lights are still burning.
- The clocks inside continue ticking.
- The front door opens before they knock.
- A mechanical servant welcomes them.

The servant politely states:

> "Master Voss is currently unavailable. Please wait until the restoration is complete."

---

# Initial Investigation

The PCs investigate the estate and workshop entrance.

---

## The Clockwork Servants

The PCs encounter simple mechanical creations performing household tasks.

Strange details:

- They avoid damaging furniture.
- They apologize when interrupted.
- They refer to rooms by personal names.
- They become distressed when called machines.

---

## The Workshop Records

The PCs discover journals describing Voss's research.

Early entries discuss:

- Clockwork engineering.
- Artificial intelligence.
- Memory preservation.

Later entries become increasingly desperate.

One final entry reads:

> "The body fails. The soul fades. But memory remains."

---

## The Family Portrait

The PCs discover a portrait of the Voss family.

The image includes:

- Alistair Voss.
- His spouse.
- His child.

The child appears identical to a small clockwork figure found elsewhere in the workshop.

---

# Act 2: The Unfinished People

## Goal

Reveal Voss's true goal and force the players to question what they are fighting.

## Runtime

45–60 minutes

---

# The Living Workshop

The PCs descend deeper into the workshop.

The machinery becomes increasingly unnatural.

The workshop itself seems to be attempting to recreate a home.

Examples:

- Mechanical servants prepare meals for nonexistent guests.
- A clockwork child asks when its parent will return.
- Machines recreate family routines from memory.

---

# The Clockwork Creations

The PCs encounter increasingly advanced constructs.

Unlike normal constructs:

They:

- Remember events.
- Express emotions.
- Fear destruction.
- Believe they are alive.

---

# The Truth Behind the Experiments

The PCs discover:

Alistair Voss was not creating weapons.

He was trying to bring his family back.

After their deaths, he discovered a method to preserve memories and personalities.

His creations are attempts to recreate the people he lost.

Each creation contains:

- Memories.
- Personality fragments.
- Emotional impressions.

But none are truly complete.

---

# Master Alistair Voss

The PCs encounter the clockmaker.

He is:

- Exhausted.
- Grieving.
- Obsessed.
- Convinced he is close to success.

He does not believe he is causing harm.

He believes:

> "If they can remember who they were, they are still here."

---

# Act 3: The Grand Assembly

## Goal

Stop Voss's final experiment.

## Runtime

75–90 minutes

---

# The Final Experiment

Voss activates his greatest creation:

A perfect mechanical recreation of his deceased child.

The machine awakens.

However:

It is incomplete.

The Memory Engine begins pulling memories from nearby creatures to finish the reconstruction.

The PCs must stop the process.

---

# Final Battlefield

## Location

The Grand Assembly Chamber.

A massive workshop containing:

- Giant gears.
- Moving platforms.
- Memory engines.
- Assembly machinery.
- Half-completed constructs.

---

# Battlefield Features

## Assembly Lines

Moving machinery transports creatures across the battlefield.

Effects:

- Forced movement.
- Separation of characters.
- Exposure to hazards.

Players can:

- Disable machinery.
- Redirect movement.
- Use it against enemies.

---

## Memory Engine

The central device powering Voss's creations.

Effects:

- Strengthens constructs.
- Creates memory-based illusions.
- Transfers emotional energy.

Players can:

- Shut it down.
- Redirect its power.
- Stabilize creations.

---

## Giant Clock Mechanism

The workshop's primary engine.

Effects:

- Rotating gears.
- Moving platforms.
- Mechanical hazards.

Players can:

- Stop the mechanism.
- Create openings.
- Cause controlled destruction.

---

## Steam Vents

Environmental hazards.

Effects:

- Areas become obscured.
- Creatures take damage.
- Movement becomes dangerous.

---

# Enemy Encounter

## Master Alistair Voss

### Role

Primary antagonist.

### Combat Style

- Uses advanced clockwork equipment.
- Commands constructs.
- Manipulates the battlefield.
- Attempts to complete the experiment.

Voss is not trying to destroy the PCs.

He is trying to stop them from ruining his life's work.

---

## The Final Prototype

### Role

Elite Construct

A nearly perfect recreation of Voss's child.

Abilities:

- Mimics memories and behaviors.
- Uses unpredictable abilities.
- Struggles between identity and programming.

---

## Supporting Enemies

### Clockwork Family Members

Incomplete creations defending Voss.

They may hesitate or question their orders.

---

# Resolution

## Success

The PCs:

- Stop the Memory Engine.
- Contain the creations.
- Resolve the Voss incident.

The Order records:

**Case File #0192 — Resolved: Artificial Life Manifestation**

---

## Alternative Resolution

The PCs determine that some of Voss's creations possess genuine consciousness.

Possible outcomes:

- The Order shelters them.
- The creations are released.
- Voss completes his research under supervision.

---

## Failure

If Voss completes the experiment:
The perfected creation awakens.
The Order discovers:
Voss did not create life.
He created something that believes it is alive.

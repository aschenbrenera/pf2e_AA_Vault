### How dangerous do you want the gameplay to be for your character?

1 — I strongly prefer low-risk gameplay.  
I want my character to be well protected from serious consequences. Failure should rarely put the character's survival in serious doubt.

2 — I prefer relatively safe gameplay.  
Characters can get into trouble, but permanent character death should be very uncommon. The GM should generally provide opportunities to recover from dangerous situations.

3 — I enjoy a moderate level of danger.  
Serious consequences are appropriate when characters make significant mistakes or take substantial risks, but character death shouldn't be routine.

4 — I enjoy high-risk gameplay.  
Characters should regularly face situations where poor decisions, failed plans, or bad luck can have serious consequences, including the possibility of character death.

5 — I want genuinely dangerous gameplay.  
Character death should be a real and meaningful possibility. I don't expect the GM to protect my character from the consequences of reckless decisions, failed plans, or dangerous situations.

### What kind of GM guided adventure do you want?

1 — Guided Tour: I prefer clear direction.  
I like the GM to provide a clear objective path and help guide the group toward what to do next.

2 — Adventure Style: I prefer narrow options and some direction.  
I like having a clear objective path, with the GM presenting meaningful choices about how to accomplish it.

3 — Campaign Style: I prefer many options and little direction.  
I like the GM to provide multiple objectives with no singular objective path, and presenting meaningful choices about how to accomplish each of them.

4 — Living World Style: I prefer player guided.  
I like having the GM to provide factions, motivations, and current events. Players decide objectives, but events move forward with or without player interaction.
